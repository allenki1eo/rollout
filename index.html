How to play

🏁Thirty marbles race down four tracks. Only the first 22 across stage 1 go through, then 15, then 8. First home in the final wins.
↔️Steer with A D or the arrow keys. On a phone, drag anywhere on the screen.
🦘Jump with Space or a quick flick up. Jump right at the lip of a ramp to clear the gap.
💨Dash with Shift for a burst of speed. It recharges after a few seconds.
🔀Some roads split into lanes. Boost lanes are fast but have a spinner. Honey lanes are safe but sticky. Bumper lanes are chaos.
🟣Purple pads launch you into the air. Pink bumpers knock you away, and blue blocks slide across the road.
🪙Grab coins on the track, often in risky spots or up in the air. Spend them on new marbles.
✨Pick an upgrade before every stage. Upgrades stack, so build a marble that suits how you race.
